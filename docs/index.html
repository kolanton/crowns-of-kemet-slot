Crowns of Kemet

A five-reel, ten-line slot built in Unity around RetroStyle Games' free Egyptian art pack. Play money only — the wallet resets, nothing is wagered.

 Coins per line bet, left to right from reel one. Wild substitutes for any paying symbol. Anubis is drawn a full column tall, so he covers a whole reel when he lands and is counted once per reel — three reels pay, times the total bet rather than the line bet. He never forms a line run.
